# Feature Mockups

Open these HTML files in your browser to see how each feature would look on your TV screens.

## Mockup Files

1. **01-qr-code-feature.html** - QR Code Integration
   - Shows how QR codes can be integrated with promos
   - Links to menu, Instagram, ordering system
   - Clean, scannable design

2. **02-live-stats.html** - Live Stats & Gamification
   - Real-time drink counts and statistics
   - Countdown timers for Happy Hour
   - Leaderboard of top drinks
   - Creates urgency and FOMO

3. **03-social-feed.html** - Social Media Feed
   - Display customer Instagram posts
   - Shows posts tagged with your hashtag
   - Encourages social sharing
   - User-generated content

4. **04-weather-promo.html** - Weather-Based Promos
   - Automatic promo based on weather
   - Rainy day = hot drinks special
   - Sunny day = frozen drinks (alternate version)
   - Fully automated, zero effort

## How to View

1. Open any HTML file in your browser
2. Press F11 for fullscreen to simulate TV view
3. The mockups are scaled to show full 1920x1080 resolution
