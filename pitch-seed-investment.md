# Venue Signage — Seed Investment Pitch

**Affordable, web-based digital signage for bars, restaurants & multi-venue operators**

*Seed investment pitch · Expand to multiple venues · Jan 2026*

---

## Vision

We build **simple, TV-ready digital signage** that runs in a browser—no extra hardware, no app stores. One proven system at a live venue (Space Clouds, Prague); we scale the same model to **dozens of venues** with the same needs: menu boards, promos, branding, and optional upsells (QR codes, live stats, weather-based offers).

---

## Problem

Bars, cafes, and restaurants want digital screens for menus and promos, but:

- **Enterprise tools** (ScreenCloud, etc.) cost $15–20+/screen/month and target large retailers.
- **DIY setups** (Raspberry Pi, USB sticks) are fragile and time-consuming to update.
- **Printed menus** go stale; owners lack time to keep screens and promos in sync.
- Most options are **software-only**—venues still need designers, video editors, and someone to run it.

Small and mid-size venues are **underserved**: they need something affordable, reliable, and **fully done for them**.

---

## Solution

**Browser-based signage** on existing TVs (e.g. LG WebOS). Hosted on the web, one short URL per screen. Works with PosterBooking or direct links. No dongles, no Raspberry Pis. Today: **3-screen layout** (logo + two menu/promo screens), looped video, fullscreen. Roadmap: scheduling, QR codes, live stats, weather-based promos.

**Full-service: we handle design, graphics, and videos.** Clients give a brief—e.g. “Make this menu,” “We need a weekly rotating menu,” “Happy Hour promo like this”—and we deliver. They don’t need designers or tech skills; we produce and deploy. Simple briefing, we take care of the rest.
